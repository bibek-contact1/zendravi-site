Zendravi Atelier

Craftsmanship

The Art of
Fragrance

At Zendravi, we believe fragrance is the most intimate form of expression. Each composition is a journey — meticulously built by perfumers who understand the language of rare ingredients.

We source the finest materials available to us: Turkish rose picked at dawn, oud from slow-grown Aquilaria, and iris butter that takes six years to mature.

The result is not simply a fragrance, but a memory waiting to be worn — one that has helped Zendravi become known as one of the best perfume houses in Nepal, from our base in Chitwan.
