Craftsmanship

The Art of
Fragrance

At Zendravi, we believe fragrance is the most intimate form of expression. Each composition is a journey—meticulously crafted by master perfumers who understand the language of rare ingredients.

We source the finest materials from around the world: Turkish roses picked at dawn, Oud from centuries-old Aquilaria trees, and Iris butter that takes six years to mature.

The result is not just a fragrance, but a memory waiting to be worn.

The Composition

Fragrance Notes

Each Zendravi fragrance is a three-act symphony, carefully composed to unfold beautifully on your skin.

Top Notes

The First Impression

Bergamot Saffron Pink Pepper Neroli

The opening chapter—bright, fleeting, and unforgettable. These notes create the initial allure that draws you in.

Heart Notes

The Soul of the Fragrance

Turkish Rose Jasmine Iris Leather

The heart unfolds slowly, revealing the true character of each composition. This is where the fragrance tells its story.

Base Notes

The Lasting Memory

Oud Amber Sandalwood Musk

The foundation—deep, warm, and enduring. These notes linger on the skin, creating a personal signature that lasts.
